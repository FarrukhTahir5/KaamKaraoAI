# KaamKarao AI — Demo Video Script 🎬
### *Duration: 3 - 5 Minutes*

This script is structured to pitch KaamKarao AI as a winning hackathon project. It focuses on the problem, demonstrates the live agentic matching lifecycle, highlights edge-case handling, and showcases the reasoning trace log.

---

## ⏱️ Video Outline & Voiceover

### 0:00 - 0:30 | The Problem
- **Visuals:** Close-up of a user scroll-storming in chaotic WhatsApp groups or struggling on a phone call seeking a plumber in G-13.
- **Narrative:**
  > "In Pakistan's massive informal economy, finding a service provider like a plumber, electrician, or tutor is chaotic. We rely on random WhatsApp recommendations, phone tag referrals, and endless manual follow-ups. There is no availability check, no trust standard, and no confirmation trail. Until now."

### 0:30 - 1:00 | The Solution (One Message Booking)
- **Visuals:** Splash screen showing KaamKarao AI, sliding into a gorgeous, clean dark slate Home Screen.
- **Narrative:**
  > "Meet KaamKarao AI. The pitch is simple: *Bas kaam batao, AI banda dhoond dega.* Instead of browsing hundreds of unverified lists, the user describes their need in a single natural sentence—using Roman Urdu, Urdu, or English."

### 1:00 - 2:00 | User Request & AI Extraction
- **Visuals:** Pressing the **AC Technician Demo** button. The input gets prefilled: *"Mujhe kal subah G-13 mein AC technician chahiye"*. Pressing search. Screen transitions to the **AI Extraction Screen** demonstrating structured fields loading in real-time.
- **Narrative:**
  > "Watch this. We click our AC Demo button: *Mujhe kal subah G-13 mein AC technician chahiye*. Instantly, our modular backend goes to work. The Intent Agent extracts the service type as 'AC Technician', the Location Agent maps G-13 sector coordinates, and the Time Parser suggested 10:00 AM tomorrow. No database browsing. The AI plans the execution steps and formats the request."

### 2:00 - 2:45 | Matching, Trust Scoring & Explainability
- **Visuals:** Navigating to the **Provider Match & Rank Screen**. Pointing out the list of matching providers, highlighting **Ali AC Services** with the golden recommended badge and its Trust Score of **92/100**. Transition to the **AI Recommendation Screen** detailing the explainability rationale.
- **Narrative:**
  > "Next, our Discovery Agent scans the registry and matches candidate providers in G-13. Then, our Ranking Agent calculates a custom Trust Score based on rating, distance, slot match, and completion rate. It highlights Ali AC Services as the top provider. And because AI shouldn't be a black box, the Decision Agent explains *exactly* why Ali is selected over competitors, contrastingly comparing Umar Cooling and Fast Cool Repair."

### 2:45 - 3:30 | Simulation Booking & Sharing
- **Visuals:** Pressing the **Confirm Booking** button. Screen shows a gorgeous confirmed booking state, complete with a Booking ID (`KK-1024`), exact estimated charges, and a WhatsApp-style teal bubble containing the shareable confirmation message.
- **Narrative:**
  > "With one click, we simulate the booking. The status is locked as 'confirmed' in our database, generating a unique booking tracking ID, and compiling a shareable WhatsApp-style message. The user can copy and share this confirmation immediately."

### 3:30 - 4:15 | Follow-up Automation Timeline
- **Visuals:** Navigating to the **Follow-Up Schedule Screen** visualizing the bulleted checklist timeline: 9:00 AM user reminder, 12:00 PM checkup, 12:15 PM review prompt.
- **Narrative:**
  > "But our system doesn't stop after the booking. The Follow-Up Agent automatically schedules reminders and completion checkups, making the entire lifecycle automated without requiring manual human checking."

### 4:15 - 5:00 | The Killer Hackathon Feature: Agent Trace Logs
- **Visuals:** Clicking **View Agent Trace Logs**. Screen renders the step-by-step logs of every single agent: Planner Agent, Intent Agent, Location Agent, Discovery, Ranking, Decision, Booking, and Follow-Up.
- **Narrative:**
  > "And here is why KaamKarao AI is a killer hackathon demo. We give users and judges complete visibility. Through our Google Antigravity Agent Trace, you can track the exact thinking, input, and output of every single module in the orchestration pipeline. This is not a static listing app—it is a truly agentic coordinator."
- **Visuals:** Outro logo screen.
- **Narrative:**
  > "KaamKarao AI. Bas kaam batao, AI banda dhoond dega. Thank you."
